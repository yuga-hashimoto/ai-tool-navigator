This report highlights the most lucrative AI affiliate programs available as of February 2026, focusing on high commission rates, recurring revenue models, and favorable cookie durations.

## 1. Speechify
*   **Best For:** Audio content, accessibility, and productivity.
*   **Commission Rate:** **50%** on all sales.
*   **Cookie Duration:** **90 days**.
*   **Why It's a Top Pick:** One of the highest percentage payouts in the industry with a generous 90-day tracking window. Speechify is a market leader in text-to-speech, making it a high-converting offer for broad audiences (students, professionals, readers).
*   **Potential Earnings:** 10 referrals to the $139/year plan = ~$695/year.

## 2. Mixo
*   **Best For:** Entrepreneurs, startups, and website builders.
*   **Commission Rate:** **50%** on all sales.
*   **Cookie Duration:** **90 days**.
*   **Why It's a Top Pick:** Exceptionally high commission for a SaaS product. Mixo's AI website builder appeals to the massive "solopreneur" market, offering a high-ticket potential with its premium plans.
*   **Potential Earnings:** 10 referrals to the ~$348/year Premium plan = ~$1,740/year.

## 3. Copy.ai
*   **Best For:** Marketers, copywriters, and agencies.
*   **Commission Rate:** **45%** of the first year's subscription.
*   **Cookie Duration:** **60 days**.
*   **Why It's a Top Pick:** A staple tool in the AI writing space with massive brand recognition. The 45% commission is significantly higher than the industry standard (typically 20-30%) for such a well-established tool.
*   **Potential Earnings:** 10 referrals to the $49/mo plan = ~$220/month (for the first year).

## 4. BasedLabs AI
*   **Best For:** Visual creators, video editors, and social media influencers.
*   **Commission Rate:** **40% Lifetime Recurring**.
*   **Cookie Duration:** **60 days**.
*   **Why It's a Top Pick:** "Lifetime recurring" is the gold standard for affiliate income. As long as your referral subscribes, you earn. BasedLabs targets the booming AI video/image niche.
*   **Potential Earnings:** High long-term passive income potential due to the recurring nature of the commission.

## 5. Homesage.ai
*   **Best For:** Real estate professionals, investors, and property managers.
*   **Commission Rate:** Up to **40% Recurring** (for 12 months).
*   **Cookie Duration:** **60 days**.
*   **Why It's a Top Pick:** A specialized, high-value niche (Real Estate AI). The 12-month recurring model offers stability, and the commission rate is top-tier for a vertical-specific SaaS.
*   **Potential Earnings:** High average order value (AOV) typical of real estate tools can lead to substantial monthly payouts.
