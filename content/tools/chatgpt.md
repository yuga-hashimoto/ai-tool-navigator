## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is an AI-powered language model developed by OpenAI. It is capable of generating human-like text based on context and past conversations.

### Key Features

*   **GPT-4:** The most powerful LLM available publicly.
*   **Plugins & browsing:** extend capabilities beyond just text.
*   **Voice Mode:** Natural conversation.

### Why use ChatGPT?

It remains the gold standard for general-purpose AI assistance, coding help, and creative writing.
