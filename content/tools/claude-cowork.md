## What is Claude Cowork?

Released in February 2026, Claude Cowork is the next evolution of AI assistants, moving from simple chat to an "AI Colleague" model. It is designed to work alongside humans by managing files, drafting complex documents, and executing multi-step business processes.

### Key Features

*   **Industry Plugins:** Specialized capabilities for Sales, Finance, Marketing, and Legal.
*   **Active File Management:** Can read, organize, and edit project directories autonomously.
*   **Agentic Execution:** Doesn't just suggest; it executes complex instructions across multiple files.

### Why choose Claude Cowork?

Claude Cowork is the premier choice for administrative efficiency. If your team needs a colleague to manage documentation, summarize long communication threads, and automate office workflows, Claude Cowork is unparalleled in its reasoning and nuance.
