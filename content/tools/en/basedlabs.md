## What is BasedLabs?

BasedLabs is an all-in-one AI content creation platform that brings together the best AI models for generating videos, images, audio, and text in a single interface. Rather than locking users into a proprietary model, BasedLabs provides access to **top-tier models from multiple providers** — including FLUX Pro Ultra, Veo 3.1, Kling 2.6 Pro, and Google's Imagen 4.

With **196+ AI tools** available and a credit-based pricing model that requires no subscription commitment, BasedLabs is positioned as a flexible hub for visual content creators who want access to the latest AI generation capabilities without managing multiple platform accounts.

## Key Features

### Multi-Model AI Video Generation

BasedLabs gives creators access to several of the leading video generation models simultaneously:
- **Veo 3.1** — Google's latest video generation model with high realism
- **Kling 2.6 Pro** — Known for smooth motion and cinematic quality
- Additional models updated as new releases become available

This lets creators choose the right model for each project rather than being limited to a single engine. High-detail product shots, cinematic scenes, and social media clips each respond differently to different models.

### AI Image Generation with FLUX Pro Ultra

For still image generation, BasedLabs features **FLUX Pro Ultra** — one of the highest-quality text-to-image models available in 2026. Users can generate images from text prompts, use image-to-image transformation, or upscale existing images to higher resolution.

### Face Swap, Voice Cloning, and AI Music

Beyond standard generation, BasedLabs includes:
- **Face Swap** — Replace faces in images and video clips for creative projects
- **Voice Cloning** — Create custom voice profiles from audio samples
- **AI Music Generation** — Generate original background music from text descriptions
- **Image Upscaling** — Enhance resolution of existing images without quality loss
- **Video Joiner** — Combine multiple clips with AI-handled transitions

### 196+ Free AI Apps Library

BasedLabs hosts an extensive library of over 196 AI tools spanning writing, copywriting, study aids, productivity, and creative applications. This makes it a broader toolkit than pure video/image platforms, useful for content teams that need AI assistance across multiple content types.

### Commercial Rights Included

All outputs from BasedLabs include **commercial usage rights** — a meaningful advantage for freelancers, agencies, and creators producing content for clients. There is no additional license fee or usage restriction for commercial applications.
