## What is Claude Cowork (Opus 4.6)?

Claude Cowork, powered by the newly released **Claude Opus 4.6 (February 2026)**, represents Anthropic's vision of a truly autonomous AI colleague. It doesn't just chat; it executes complex, multi-step workflows across your entire software stack.

### Key Features of Opus 4.6

*   **Advanced Agent Teams:** Deploy specialized sub-agents that collaborate on large-scale tasks, from legal research to full-stack debugging.
*   **1M Context Window:** Claude can now "hold" an entire medium-sized codebase or a year's worth of financial data in its active memory.
*   **Agentic Search & Reasoning:** Leads the industry in benchmarks like GPQA Diamond and BigLaw, making it the top choice for professional services.

### Claude Cowork vs. Google Antigravity

With the 4.6 update, Claude Cowork has narrowed the gap in **agentic coding**. While Google Antigravity remains the specialist for production-grade system maintenance and A2UI-native development, Claude Cowork is now the preferred tool for **business-critical reasoning**, legal/financial analysis, and high-level project planning.

### Why Use It for Monetization?

For entrepreneurs building content platforms or professional service agencies, Claude Cowork Opus 4.6 allows you to automate the reasoning layer of your business. It can write, research, and audit your sites with a level of nuance that previously required senior human oversight.
