## What is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai has evolved from a simple writing assistant into a full-fledged **GTM (Go-to-Market) AI Platform** (which recently acquired Fullcast). It helps sales, marketing, and operations teams automate complex workflows, from prospecting to lead enrichment, using advanced AI agents.

### Key Features

*   **GTM Workflows:** Automate repetitive go-to-market tasks such as sales prospecting, inbound lead processing, and account-based marketing.
*   **Copy Agents:** Deploy autonomous AI agents that can generate, review, and refine content based on your unique brand voice.
*   **Intelligence Layer & Tables:** Manage unified data strategies and CRM enrichment to ensure your GTM teams have the best context.
*   **Multi-Team Focus:** Solutions specifically designed for Sales, Marketing, and Operations.

### Who is it for?

Copy.ai is now built for scaling businesses and enterprise GTM teams who want to automate their sales and marketing operations. While it still handles content creation, its true power lies in streamlining complex pipelines and boosting team productivity.
