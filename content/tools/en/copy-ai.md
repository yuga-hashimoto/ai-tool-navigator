## What is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai has evolved significantly from its origins as an AI copywriting tool. In 2026, it is positioned as a **GTM (Go-To-Market) AI Platform** — an automation engine designed to streamline the entire revenue operation: prospecting, lead enrichment, content creation, outreach personalization, and sales enablement.

The platform is LLM-agnostic, meaning it dynamically selects the best available large language model — from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google — for each specific task. This gives Copy.ai flexibility to optimize for quality or cost depending on the use case without locking users into a single AI provider.

---

## Key Features

### Workflows — Multi-Step GTM Automation

The core differentiator is **Workflows**: automated, multi-step processes that can span different teams and tools. A workflow might automatically research a prospect, generate personalized outreach email, add it to a Salesforce sequence, and notify the SDR — all without manual intervention. This is where Copy.ai moves beyond content generation into actual process automation.

### Brand Voice and Infobase

**Brand Voice** analyzes your website or existing content to learn your company's specific tone, style, and messaging. **Infobase** serves as a central knowledge hub that gives the AI context about your products, customers, and value propositions. Together, they ensure that generated content sounds like your brand, not generic AI output.

### LLM-Agnostic Model Selection

Copy.ai doesn't rely on a single AI provider. It uses models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini — selecting the best model for each task type. This means you benefit from advances across all major AI providers without needing to manage multiple AI subscriptions or switch platforms.

### 90+ Content Templates

For teams that need to produce content quickly without building custom workflows, Copy.ai includes over **90 templates** for common marketing and sales assets: email sequences, social posts, ad copy, blog outlines, landing page copy, product descriptions, and more.

### 2,000+ Integrations

Through Zapier and direct API connectors, Copy.ai integrates with over **2,000 applications**. Direct integrations include Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, and major email platforms — enabling workflow automation without custom development for most standard sales and marketing tech stacks.

## Who Should Use Copy.ai?

**Best for:**
- Marketing teams that need to produce large volumes of consistent, on-brand content
- Sales teams looking to automate prospect research, personalization, and outreach sequences
- Revenue operations teams building end-to-end GTM automation
- Small businesses that need a $29/month entry point to AI content tools

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You need high-quality long-form content without extensive editing — dedicated AI writing tools like Jasper may suit better
- Your primary use case is simple, one-off copywriting — simpler tools have lower overhead
- You need a dialer, visitor ID, or multi-channel outreach beyond email and LinkedIn
