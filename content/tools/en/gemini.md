## What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is Google DeepMind's family of multimodal AI models, designed from the ground up to reason across text, code, audio, images, and video simultaneously. Google released **Gemini 3** in January 2026 with major advances in agentic capabilities and reasoning performance.

The flagship model for developers and power users is **Gemini 3.1 Pro**, which scores **80.6% on SWE-Bench Verified** — the standard benchmark for real-world software engineering tasks — placing it among the highest-performing coding AI models available. It also powers Google's **Antigravity IDE**, the agentic development environment Google launched in late 2025.

---

## Key Features

### State-of-the-Art Coding Performance

Gemini 3.1 Pro's 80.6% SWE-Bench Verified score represents a major leap in software engineering capability. This translates to real results on production codebases: complex multi-file refactors, dependency analysis, and architecture-level reasoning that previously required significant human guidance.

### 1 Million Token Context Window

Gemini 3.1 Pro supports a **1 million token context window**, enabling you to load entire codebases, hour-long video transcripts, or large financial reports into a single prompt. This is particularly powerful for code review at scale, long-form document analysis, and cross-referencing multiple large sources simultaneously.

### Native Multimodality

Unlike models that have multimodal capabilities added on top, Gemini was built multimodal from inception. It can analyze screenshots alongside code, interpret diagrams in documentation, process audio recordings, and reason across all these modalities in a single context window — without losing coherence between the different content types.

### Google Workspace Integration

Gemini integrates directly with Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive. For teams already in the Google ecosystem, this means AI assistance is available in the tools they already use daily, without needing to copy-paste content into a separate AI interface.

### Antigravity IDE Integration

Gemini 3.1 Pro is the primary model powering **Google Antigravity**, the agentic coding IDE Google launched in 2025. If you use Antigravity for development, you are already using Gemini's capabilities through a purpose-built interface optimized for software engineering workflows.

### Multiple Tiers for Different Use Cases

The Gemini family includes models at different capability and cost levels:
- **Gemini Nano** — on-device, privacy-preserving
- **Gemini Flash** — fast, cost-efficient for high-volume applications
- **Gemini Pro** — balanced performance for most use cases
- **Gemini Ultra** — maximum capability for the most demanding tasks

## Who Should Use Gemini?

**Best for:**
- Developers who want a coding AI tightly integrated with Google's ecosystem (AI Studio, Antigravity, Firebase)
- Teams that work heavily with Google Workspace and want AI assistance within familiar tools
- Use cases requiring multimodal reasoning — analyzing images, video, or audio alongside text
- Engineers who need a 1M token context window for large codebase or document analysis

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You are not in the Google ecosystem and want simpler pricing
- Your primary need is conversational AI without heavy multimodal requirements
