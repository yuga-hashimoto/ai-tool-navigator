## What is Gemini?

Gemini is a family of multimodal AI models developed by Google DeepMind. It is built from the ground up to be multimodal, which means it can generalize and seamlessly understand, operate across, and combine different types of information including text, code, audio, image, and video.

### Key Features

*   **Multimodal Reasoning:** Sophisticated reasoning capabilities.
*   **Coding:** Advanced coding skills in popular languages.
*   **Scalable:** Different sizes for different tasks (Nano, Flash, Pro, Ultra).

### Why use Gemini?

If you are already in the Google ecosystem, Gemini offers seamless integration. Its multimodal capabilities are also top-tier, making it excellent for analyzing images and videos alongside text.
