## What is Grok 3?

Grok 3 is the flagship AI model from **xAI**, Elon Musk's AI company, trained on the Colossus GPU cluster — one of the largest AI training systems ever built. It combines strong reasoning capabilities with a unique competitive advantage: **real-time access to the full X (formerly Twitter) data stream**, giving it information currency that training-cutoff-based models cannot match.

The model scored **93.3% on the AIME 2025** mathematics competition, placing it among the strongest reasoning models available. xAI also offers multiple Grok variants with context windows up to **2 million tokens**.

---

## Key Features

### Real-Time X Data Integration

Grok's most distinctive feature is live integration with X's data. This means Grok can reference tweets, trending topics, breaking news, and real-time public conversations — not just information from its training cutoff. For research, market analysis, or staying current on fast-moving technical topics, this is a meaningful advantage over models with static knowledge.

### DeepSearch

DeepSearch is Grok's enhanced research mode that searches and synthesizes information from both the web and X simultaneously. It is designed for complex queries that require pulling together multiple sources and reasoning across them — useful for competitive analysis, technical research, or any task where breadth of current information matters.

### Exceptional Mathematical Reasoning

Grok 3's 93.3% score on AIME 2025 reflects strong mathematical and logical reasoning capabilities. For developers, this translates to better performance on algorithm design, debugging complex logic, and working through mathematical proofs or formal specifications.

### 2 Million Token Context Window

Grok 3 supports up to **2 million tokens** of context, which is among the largest context windows available in 2026. This enables loading very large codebases, lengthy research documents, or extended conversation histories without truncation.

### Aurora Image Generation

Grok includes **Aurora**, xAI's image generation model, accessible through the free and paid tiers. Aurora handles creative image generation with fewer content restrictions than some competitors.

xAI's API pricing is deliberately low to drive adoption and volume. For developers building applications that need real-time information or strong reasoning at scale, the cost-to-performance ratio is compelling.
