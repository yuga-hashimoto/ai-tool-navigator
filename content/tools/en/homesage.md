## What is Homesage?

Homesage is an AI-powered real estate intelligence platform that gives investors, agents, and developers access to deep property analytics across more than **140 million US residential properties**. Rather than relying on general market sentiment, Homesage uses machine learning — including computer vision — to surface investment-grade insights that manual research cannot practically produce.

The platform collects off-market and MLS listings daily, applies proprietary AI models to each property, and generates investment indicators that help users identify opportunities, assess risk, and price negotiations with data rather than instinct.

---

## Key Features

### Investment Property Search

Homesage's property search goes beyond basic filters. It incorporates AI-generated investment indicators — proprietary metrics that combine price trajectory, rental yield potential, neighborhood trajectory, and comparable sales into a single investment readiness score. This allows investors to screen large volumes of properties quickly and focus due diligence where the data signals opportunity.

### Full Property Reports

For any property in the database, Homesage generates a comprehensive AI report covering:
- Investment potential score (neural network-generated)
- Price Negotiability Model — how much flexibility exists in the asking price
- Renovation cost estimates with ROI projections
- Rental income analysis and NOI assessment
- IRR (Internal Rate of Return) calculations for different hold periods
- Comparable sales analysis

### Price Negotiability Model

Introduced in July 2025, the Price Negotiability Model is a standout feature. It analyzes listing history, days on market, seller behavior patterns, and comparable data to estimate exactly how much room exists in a given asking price. For buyers and agents, this replaces guesswork with data-backed negotiation strategy.

### Developer-Friendly APIs

For teams building real estate applications, Homesage offers a RESTful API with **20+ endpoints** and sub-100ms response times. Endpoints cover property search, investment scoring, renovation estimates, rental analysis, and market data — all accessible with transparent, usage-based pricing. The API integrates cleanly with CRMs, investment platforms, and property management tools.

### AI-Powered CRM and Automation

Homesage includes tools for automating lead nurturing and qualification, digital document generation, and transaction milestone tracking. For real estate agents, this reduces administrative overhead and keeps pipelines moving with less manual follow-up.

## Who Should Use Homesage?

**Best for:**
- Real estate investors who want data-driven property screening at scale
- Agents who want to provide clients with AI-backed investment analysis
- Developers building real estate applications and need reliable property data APIs
- Property managers looking to automate CRM and transaction workflows

**Consider alternatives if:**
- Your focus is outside the US — coverage is primarily domestic
- You need agent-facing CRM with deep pipeline management (dedicated real estate CRMs may serve better)
- Your investment thesis is based on hyperlocal market knowledge that data alone can't capture
