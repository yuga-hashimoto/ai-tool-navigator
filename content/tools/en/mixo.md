## What is Mixo?

Mixo is an AI-powered website builder designed to help entrepreneurs and startups validate their ideas quickly. With just a brief description of your startup idea, Mixo generates a complete landing page with copy, images, and a waiting list form in seconds.

### Key Features

*   **Instant Website Generation:** Describe your idea, and Mixo's AI builds a professional-looking website instantly.
*   **Email Collection:** Built-in tools to capture leads and grow your audience from day one.
*   **Editor:** Easy-to-use editor to customize text, images, and colors without any coding.
*   **SEO Optimized:** Websites are optimized for search engines to help you get found online.

### Who is it for?

Mixo is ideal for solopreneurs, indie hackers, and anyone with a startup idea who wants to test the market without spending weeks on development. It's the fastest way to launch a landing page and start collecting emails.
