## What is Mixo?

Mixo is an AI-powered website builder designed specifically for entrepreneurs, indie hackers, and startups who need to launch quickly. Describe your business or idea in plain language and Mixo generates a complete, professional-looking website — with copy, images, layout, and an email collection form — in seconds.

Trusted by over **3 million businesses worldwide**, Mixo has found its niche as the fastest way to go from idea to live landing page. It is purpose-built for the specific challenge of validating a new product or service: getting a professional presence online before you have a product ready, collecting email signups, and testing whether there is market interest.

---

## Key Features

### AI Website Generation from Text

The core feature is simple but powerful: describe your business in 1–2 sentences, and Mixo's AI generates a complete website. This includes:
- Professional copy that communicates your value proposition clearly
- Relevant imagery matched to your business category
- A structured layout that guides visitors toward your goal
- An email signup form configured and ready to collect leads

The entire process takes under a minute. You can then use the built-in editor to customize text, images, and colors without any technical skill.

### Built-In Email Collection and Lead Management

From the moment your site launches, Mixo captures visitor emails through the integrated waiting list form. Collected subscribers are managed within the Mixo dashboard — you can view, export, and message your list directly. For early-stage products, this is the primary use case: building an audience before the product exists.

### Custom Domain and SSL

All paid plans include custom domain connection and SSL certificates — the basics required for a professional web presence. Hosting is served through a global content delivery network (CDN) for fast load times regardless of visitor location.

### Multi-Page Sites (Growth and Premium)

While the Basic plan supports a single-page site, the Growth plan allows up to **5 pages** and the Premium plan supports up to **50 pages** — enabling more complete websites as your product matures beyond the initial landing page.

### AI-Powered Page Expansion

Beyond the initial generation, Mixo can generate additional pages using AI. Describe what you need — an About page, a Features page, a Blog — and Mixo produces the content. This keeps the AI-assisted workflow intact as your site grows.

## Who Should Use Mixo?

**Best for:**
- Founders testing a startup idea and needing a landing page in under an hour
- Indie hackers validating products with a pre-launch waitlist
- Freelancers and consultants who need a professional web presence quickly without budget for a developer
- Anyone running a beta test or waiting list campaign

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You need ecommerce functionality — Shopify or Squarespace Commerce are better options
- You need deep CRM integration or marketing automation
- Your site needs complex functionality (booking systems, user accounts, member areas)

## Bottom Line

Mixo is the best tool for one specific job: getting a professional website live in minutes to capture early interest and validate ideas. The 3M+ user base reflects its success at this focused use case. At $9–$39/month, it is one of the most affordable paths from idea to live website. If you need more than a landing page or simple informational site, plan to migrate to a more capable platform as your product grows.
