## What is Semrush?

Semrush is the industry's most comprehensive online visibility management platform. It covers every dimension of digital marketing: SEO, paid advertising (PPC), content marketing, social media, and competitive intelligence — all from a single dashboard.

Founded in 2008 and now trusted by over 10 million marketing professionals, Semrush has built the **world's largest keyword database** with over 20 billion keywords, making it the reference tool for serious SEO and content strategy work. In 2026, it expanded further with **Semrush One** — a combined plan that integrates traditional SEO tools with AI Visibility tracking, the new frontier of monitoring how brands appear in AI-generated search results.

---

## Key Features

### Keyword Magic Tool — 20 Billion Keywords

The Keyword Magic Tool is the foundation of Semrush's SEO offering. It provides access to over **20 billion keywords** with search volume, keyword difficulty scores, intent classification, CPC data, and SERP feature analysis. You can filter by question format, long-tail variations, or semantic clusters, making it powerful for both discovery and strategy.

### Site Audit — Technical SEO at Scale

Semrush's Site Audit crawls your website and identifies technical SEO issues across over 130 checks: broken links, crawl errors, duplicate content, Core Web Vitals issues, HTTPS problems, and more. It prioritizes issues by severity and provides clear recommendations — reducing the need for a dedicated technical SEO consultant for most sites.

### Competitor Traffic & Strategy Analysis

Enter any competitor's domain and Semrush reveals their organic traffic estimates, top-performing pages, keyword rankings, backlink profile, and paid advertising strategies. The **Market Explorer** feature extends this to full industry analysis — identifying market leaders, new entrants, and traffic trends across entire verticals.

### AI-Powered Content Tools

The **Content Toolkit** generates SEO-optimized content outlines and drafts based on real ranking data. At higher plan tiers, content generation is unlimited. The **SEO Writing Assistant** provides real-time guidance as you write, scoring your content against the top 10 ranking pages for your target keyword.

### AI Visibility Tracking (Semrush One)

The new **Semrush One** plan ($199/month) combines traditional SEO metrics with **AI Visibility tracking** — monitoring how your brand and content appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and other LLM-powered search tools. As AI search becomes a significant traffic source, this is increasingly critical for brand visibility.

### Backlink Analysis & Link Building

Semrush's backlink database covers trillions of links and updates continuously. The **Link Building Tool** identifies outreach opportunities, tracks link acquisition campaigns, and monitors competitor link building activity — all within the platform.

## Who Should Use Semrush?

**Best for:**
- SEO agencies managing multiple client accounts
- In-house marketing teams at growth-stage or enterprise companies
- Content marketers who need keyword research and content optimization in one tool
- Anyone who needs to deeply understand competitor digital strategies

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You are a beginner blogger on a budget — Ahrefs Lite or Ubersuggest are more accessible starting points
- Your SEO needs are basic and don't require competitive intelligence depth
