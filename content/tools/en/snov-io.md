## What is Snov.io?

Snov.io is a comprehensive B2B sales intelligence and cold outreach automation platform. It covers every step of the outbound sales process: finding prospects, verifying their contact information, building automated email sequences, and managing the resulting pipeline — all from a single platform.

The database covers over **50 million company profiles**, searchable through 15 different filters including industry, company size, location, technology stack, and revenue range. For sales teams that depend on outbound prospecting, this eliminates the need to stitch together multiple separate tools for data sourcing, verification, outreach, and CRM management.

---

## Key Features

### Email Finder — Domain and Social Lookup

Snov.io's Email Finder locates corporate email addresses using multiple methods:
- **Domain search**: Enter a company domain and find all associated email addresses
- **Name + company search**: Find the email for a specific person at a specific company
- **LinkedIn Chrome Extension**: Export contact information directly from LinkedIn profiles and search results
- **Bulk CSV upload**: Find emails for large lists at scale

The platform surfaces the most likely email format based on verified patterns, reducing guesswork in outreach campaigns.

### Email Verifier — Reduce Bounce Rates

Before sending any campaign, Snov.io verifies every email address against multiple signals: SMTP validation, domain verification, catch-all detection, and role-based address flagging. This process significantly reduces bounce rates and protects sender reputation — a critical factor for email deliverability in 2026 when inbox placement has become harder.

### Email Warm-Up

Snov.io includes email warm-up functionality that gradually increases sending volume and engagement signals for new email accounts. This is essential for maintaining deliverability when scaling outreach or onboarding new sales team members. Up to three warm-up accounts are included; unlimited warm-ups require the $74/month Pro plan.

### Automated Email Campaigns with AI Personalization

The campaign builder supports multi-step sequences with conditional logic: if a prospect opens but doesn't reply, send a different follow-up than if they don't open at all. AI-powered personalization can incorporate prospect-specific details (company, role, recent news) into each message automatically, improving open and reply rates compared to generic templates.

### Free Native CRM

Snov.io includes a **fully functional CRM at no extra cost**, with support for up to 100 pipeline stages and deal tracking. For teams that don't want to pay separately for Salesforce or HubSpot, this is a meaningful included value. For teams already using an existing CRM, Snov.io integrates via Zapier and direct API.

### LinkedIn Automation (Add-On)

LinkedIn outreach — profile visits, connection requests, and direct messages — is available as a **separate add-on at $69/month per LinkedIn slot**. This is not included in any base plan pricing and should be factored into total cost calculations for teams doing multi-channel outreach.

## Pricing (2026)

| Plan | Monthly | Key Features |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Starter | $30/mo | Email finder, verifier, basic campaigns, free CRM |
| Pro | $74/mo | A/B testing, unlimited warm-ups, advanced personalization |
| Business | $149/mo | Higher volume limits, team management |
| Enterprise | $277/mo | Maximum limits, dedicated support |
| LinkedIn Add-On | $69/mo per slot | LinkedIn profile visits, connections, messages |

Snov.io's pricing appears simple, but the actual cost depends on usage patterns. Heavy prospecting depletes credits faster than expected, and the LinkedIn add-on is separate. Teams doing serious multi-channel SDR work should budget $1,000–$3,000+/month when factoring in all components.

## Who Should Use Snov.io?

**Best for:**
- B2B sales teams that rely on outbound prospecting and cold email
- Small to mid-size companies looking for an all-in-one outreach platform without enterprise pricing
- Teams that want a free CRM bundled with their outreach tools
- SDRs and BDRs who need LinkedIn prospecting alongside email campaigns

**Consider alternatives if:**
- You need a built-in dialer, visitor identification, or social selling channels beyond LinkedIn
- Your company has strict compliance requirements around cold outreach at scale
- You need deep CRM functionality that goes beyond pipeline management

## Bottom Line

Snov.io is one of the most complete cold outreach platforms available at accessible pricing. The Starter plan at $30/month gives small teams a solid foundation for email prospecting, and the included CRM adds genuine value. For teams scaling outbound seriously, the Pro plan at $74/month is the practical minimum — and LinkedIn automation should be budgeted as a separate line item.
