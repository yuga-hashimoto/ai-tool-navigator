## What is Snov.io?

Snov.io is a specialized platform for B2B sales and lead generation. It provides tools to find leads, verify their email addresses, and automate cold outreach campaigns at scale.

### Key Features

*   **Email Finder:** Find corporate email addresses from domains or social profiles.
*   **Email Verifier:** Reduce bounce rates by checking if emails are valid.
*   **Email Campaigns:** Create automated, personalized follow-up sequences.
*   **Li Prospector:** Export leads directly from LinkedIn.

### Why choose Snov.io?

If your business relies on outbound sales, Snov.io automates the most time-consuming parts of the process, allowing you to focus on closing deals.
