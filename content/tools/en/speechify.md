## What is Speechify?

Speechify is the #1 AI text-to-speech app that reads any text aloud for you. It uses advanced AI to generate natural-sounding voices that make listening to documents, articles, and books easier and more enjoyable.

### Key Features

*   **Natural Voices:** Listen to text read by AI voices that sound human, including celebrity voices like Gwyneth Paltrow and Snoop Dogg.
*   **Cross-Platform:** Available as a Chrome extension, iOS app, Android app, and Mac app, so you can listen anywhere.
*   **Speed Control:** Adjust the reading speed up to 900 words per minute to consume content faster.
*   **OCR Technology:** Scan physical books or documents and have them read aloud instantly.

### Who is it for?

Speechify is perfect for students with reading difficulties like dyslexia, professionals who want to multitask, and anyone who prefers listening to reading. It helps improve comprehension and retention while saving time.
