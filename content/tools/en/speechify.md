## What is Speechify?

Speechify is the world's leading AI text-to-speech platform, with over **55 million users** as of 2026. It converts any text — documents, PDFs, websites, emails, books — into natural-sounding audio, enabling users to consume written content by listening rather than reading.

The platform is particularly popular with students who have dyslexia or other reading difficulties, busy professionals who want to multitask, and anyone looking to increase their content consumption speed. At up to **900 words per minute** (adjustable), power users can work through material far faster than reading allows.

---

## Key Features

### 1000+ Natural AI Voices

Speechify Premium unlocks over **1,000 AI voices** across 60+ languages. Voice quality has improved dramatically — voices sound natural and expressive, not robotic. The platform also includes a selection of **celebrity voices**, including Gwyneth Paltrow, Snoop Dogg, and others, which makes listening to documents a less monotonous experience.

### Variable Speed Up to 900 WPM

The ability to adjust playback speed is central to Speechify's value proposition. While the free plan caps at 1.5x, Premium unlocks up to **900 words per minute**. Most users settle in the 2x–3x range for efficient listening; power users who train themselves at higher speeds report dramatically increased information consumption rates.

### OCR — Read Physical Documents

Speechify's **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)** technology lets you scan physical books, printed articles, handwritten notes, or any paper document with your phone camera and have it read aloud instantly. This is particularly useful for students working with textbooks or professionals dealing with printed contracts or reports.

### Cross-Platform Availability

Speechify works as:

- **Chrome extension** — reads any webpage or imported document
- **iOS app** — read PDFs, ebooks, and documents on iPhone/iPad
- **Android app** — full feature parity with iOS
- **Mac app** — desktop listening with background playback

All platforms sync your library and settings, so you can start listening on desktop and continue on mobile without losing your place.

### AI Features (Premium)

Premium includes AI-powered summaries, which condense long documents into key points before or after listening. The **AI Podcasts** feature creates audio briefings from your document library, and the **Voice AI Assistant** enables conversational interaction with your documents.

### Speechify Studio — Voiceover Production

Speechify Studio is a separate product for professional voiceover creation, using Speechify's voices to produce audio content for videos, podcasts, or training materials. Pricing is credit-based: Free (600 credits), Starter ($19/month for ~2 hours of audio), Creator ($49/month for ~8 hours).

## Who Should Use Speechify?

**Best for:**

- Students with dyslexia, ADHD, or other reading difficulties who benefit from audio learning
- Professionals who want to consume reports, emails, or articles while commuting or exercising
- Researchers and content creators who need to process large volumes of text efficiently
- Anyone producing voiceover content who needs high-quality AI voices

**Consider alternatives if:**

- Your needs are simple — free text-to-speech tools built into browsers or operating systems may suffice
- You primarily need voiceover for professional video production and want more control over voice customization
