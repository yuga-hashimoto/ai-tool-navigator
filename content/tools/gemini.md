## What is Gemini 3 Pro?

Gemini 3 Pro is the latest iteration of Google DeepMind's flagship multimodal AI model family. Released in early 2026, it introduces groundbreaking capabilities in reasoning, coding, and long-context understanding. It is built from the ground up to be multimodal, seamlessly operating across text, code, audio, image, and video.

### Key Features (2026 Updates)

*   **Antigravity Coding:** The new "Antigravity" feature allows Gemini to act as an autonomous coding agent, capable of managing complex projects directly within your IDE.
*   **Multimodal Reasoning:** Significantly improved reasoning capabilities compared to Gemini 1.5 Pro.
*   **Ultra-Low Latency:** Optimized for real-time interactions, making it feel instantaneous.
*   **Scalable:** Available in Nano (on-device), Flash (fast & efficient), Pro (best overall), and Ultra (most capable) sizes.

### Why use Gemini 3 Pro?

If you are a developer, the **Antigravity** integration is a game-changer. It transforms Gemini from a chat assistant into a full-fledged pair programmer. For general users, its deep integration with Google Workspace and superior multimodal understanding make it the best all-around AI assistant currently available.
