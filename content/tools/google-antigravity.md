## What is Google Antigravity?

Google Antigravity is the "Agent-First" IDE launched in early 2026. Designed by Google DeepMind, it replaces traditional linear coding with an agentic workflow where Gemini 3 Pro acts as a project manager for multiple sub-agents.

### Key Features

*   **Artifacts System:** Real-time preview and interaction with AI-generated assets.
*   **Agentic Search:** Deep semantic search across global repositories and internal docs.
*   **One-Click Deployment:** Seamless integration with GCP for instant scaling.

### Why use Google Antigravity?

If you are building in the Google ecosystem, Antigravity offers the most integrated and powerful agentic development experience currently available in 2026.
