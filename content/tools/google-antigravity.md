## What is Google Antigravity?

Google Antigravity is an AI-native integrated development environment (IDE) built from the ground up to support the future of agentic coding. It leverages the full power of Gemini 3 Ultra and Pro to provide a coding experience where the AI is a first-class participant in the development process.

Originally launched in late 2025 and released to public preview in early 2026, Antigravity marks a significant return to "Founder Mode" for Google, with co-founder Sergey Brin directly involved in refining its agentic capabilities.

### Key Features

*   **Native Agentic Framework:** Built-in tools for spawning and managing sub-agents to handle specific tasks.
*   **Production-Grade Reliability:** Recognized for its ability to safely handle production databases and ship complex products.
*   **A2UI (Agent-to-UI):** Revolutionary interface that allows agents to present and manipulate UI elements directly.
*   **Generous Free Tier:** Unlike competitors like Cursor (Kiro) or Windsurf, Antigravity offers substantial free access to Gemini 3 Pro for developers.

### Google Antigravity vs. Claude Code/Cowork vs. Cursor

While Claude Cowork excels at document summarization and general documentation management, Google Antigravity is the tool of choice for fixing production-level issues and shipping products. Its integration into the Google AI ecosystem provides a level of scale and reliability that is difficult to match in high-stakes environments. Compared to Cursor (or Kiro), Antigravity's deep integration with Gemini models offers unique advantages in context handling and reasoning speed.
