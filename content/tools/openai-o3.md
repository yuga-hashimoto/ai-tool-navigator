## What is OpenAI o3?

OpenAI o3, released in early 2026, is the successor to the o1 series. It introduces advanced reflective deliberation, allowing the model to "think" longer and verify its own logic before responding to complex prompts.

### Key Features

*   **Deep Reasoning:** Handles multi-step mathematical and logical proofs with unprecedented accuracy.
*   **Self-Correction:** Internal loops to identify and fix errors during the reasoning process.
*   **Agentic Hooks:** Designed to be the "brain" for higher-level agentic architectures.

### Why use OpenAI o3?

When accuracy and logical rigor are more important than speed, o3 is the market leader for complex problem-solving and research tasks.
