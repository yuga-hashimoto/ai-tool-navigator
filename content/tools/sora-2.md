## OpenAI Sora 2 Discontinuation Notice

**Important Update:** The Sora web and app experiences were officially discontinued on **April 26, 2026**. Furthermore, the Sora API is scheduled to be discontinued on **September 24, 2026**.

### Data Export and Access

If you used Sora previously, you have options to retrieve your work:

- **Export Content:** You can export content you created in Sora by navigating to `sora.chatgpt.com/sunset` and clicking on the Export button. You will receive an email once your export is ready.
- **Export Window:** We recommend you export your Sora content as soon as possible. If a final export window is offered, OpenAI will notify users via email before it begins.
- **Data Deletion:** After Sora is fully discontinued, and following the period of any final export window, OpenAI will permanently delete any data associated with your use of Sora.

### Refunds

For refund questions related to any purchases made prior to the discontinuation, please refer to the OpenAI Help Center article on requesting a refund for a ChatGPT subscription.
