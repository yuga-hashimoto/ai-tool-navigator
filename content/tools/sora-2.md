## What is OpenAI Sora 2?

> **⚠️ Discontinuation Notice:** The Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026. The Sora API will be discontinued on September 24, 2026.

Released in September 2025, **Sora 2** represents the pinnacle of generative video technology. Building on the foundation of the original Sora, this iteration solves the critical issues of **object permanence** and **audio integration**, making it less of a video generator and more of a reality simulator.

### Key Features for Commercial Use

- **Native Audio:** Sora 2 doesn't just generate video; it generates the corresponding audio simultaneously. Footsteps on gravel sound like gravel, and dialogue (though still experimental) matches lip movements with surprising accuracy.
- **Physics Engine:** The model understands complex physical interactions. Liquids behave correctly, glass shatters realistically, and light reflects off surfaces based on material properties.
- **Character Consistency:** The new "Character Cameo" feature allows creators to define a character and reuse them across different scenes and shots, a game-changer for narrative filmmaking.

### Monetization Potential

Sora 2 is designed for high-end production:

1.  **Filmmaking:** Indie filmmakers can now generate complex VFX shots or entire scenes that would previously require a dedicated team.
2.  **Sound Design:** The integrated audio generation saves time and money on Foley artists and sound libraries.
3.  **Virtual Production:** Create photorealistic backgrounds for LED volumes in real-time production environments.

### Pricing & Commercial Rights

Sora 2 operates on a tiered model.

- **ChatGPT Plus:** Includes limited access (standard generation, no commercial license for high-res).
- **Sora Pro ($200/mo):** The serious commercial tier. Unlocks unlimited generations, 4K resolution, and a comprehensive commercial license for all outputs.

### Verdict

Sora 2 is the **Physicist's Choice**. If you need absolute realism and integrated audio, there is simply no other tool that comes close. It is expensive, but for professional studios, it replaces tens of thousands of dollars in traditional production costs.
