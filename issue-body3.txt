## Summary
Implement comprehensive email personalization engine for marketing campaigns.

## Goals
- Dynamic content based on user behavior and preferences
- Behavioral triggers for personalized emails
- Segmentation-based email targeting
- A/B testing for content optimization
- Personalized subject lines and CTAs

## Technical Requirements
- Email personalization engine with dynamic content
- User attribute mapping and triggers
- Email template system with placeholders
- Segmentation rules engine
- Analytics for email performance by personalization

## Expected Impact
- 20-30% increase in email open rates
- 15-25% increase in click-through rates
- 10-15% increase in conversion rates
- 5-10% increase in revenue per email

## Success Metrics
- Personalization adoption rate
- Email engagement by segment
- Personalization vs generic email performance
- Content conversion rate
- A/B test performance

## Implementation Phases
1. User attribute data collection
2. Personalization engine and triggers
3. Email template system
4. Segmentation rules
5. Analytics and optimization
