## Summary
Implement dynamic pricing optimization system that adjusts prices based on demand and competition.

## Goals
- Real-time price optimization based on demand signals
- Competitor price monitoring and adjustment
- A/B testing for optimal pricing
- Dynamic pricing rules engine
- Price change notifications to users

## Technical Requirements
- Demand signal collection and analysis
- Competitor price monitoring API
- Dynamic pricing algorithm
- A/B testing framework
- Price change management and rollback system

## Expected Impact
- 10-20% increase in revenue per user
- 5-15% increase in conversion rate
- 15-25% improvement in profit margin

## Success Metrics
- Dynamic pricing revenue impact
- Price change frequency and magnitude
- Conversion rate by price tier
- Customer response to price changes
- Profit margin improvement

## Implementation Phases
1. Demand signal collection system
2. Competitor monitoring implementation
3. Pricing algorithm development
4. A/B testing framework
5. Management interface and monitoring
